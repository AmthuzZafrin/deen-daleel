# Do I have to say bismillah before wudu?

## Answer

Say it. On the majority position you have not lost your wudu if you forget, and
even on the strictest one, forgetting is excused.

**The majority say it is recommended.** Ibn Qudama: **the apparent position of
Ahmad is that the basmala is a sunna in every purification from minor
impurity** — and he lists the company: **this is the position of al-Thawri,
Malik, al-Shafi'i, Abu 'Ubayda, Ibn al-Mundhir and the people of opinion.** [1]

**A minority say it is obligatory.** The same passage: **and from him [Ahmad]
that it is obligatory in all of them — wudu, ghusl and tayammum** [2] — chosen by
al-Khallal, and the position of al-Hasan and Ishaq. Their evidence is the hadith:
**there is no wudu for one who does not mention God's name over it.** [3]

**Now the part that decides it, and it is a piece of hadith criticism from the
same Imam whose school is being described.** Ibn Qudama quotes Ahmad on that
hadith: **no hadith is established in this, and I know of none in it with a good
chain.** [4] Al-Tirmidhi records Ahmad saying the same thing in his own
collection. Ibn Qudama then gives the consequence: **if it is sound, it is
understood as emphasising the recommendation and negating completeness without
it** [5] — the same way "there is no prayer for a mosque's neighbour except in
the mosque" is read.

This is worth pausing on. The strongest text for the obligatory position is one
the leading authority of the school that holds it declared unestablished. That is
how the tradition argues, and it is why the majority landed where it did.

**If you forget.** Ibn Qudama records the question put to Ahmad directly: **I
asked Ahmad b. Hanbal about someone who forgets the basmala in wudu. He said: I
hope there is nothing on him.** [6] Al-Tirmidhi reports Ishaq — who held it
obligatory — drawing the same line: **if he leaves the basmala deliberately he
repeats the wudu; if he was forgetful or interpreting, it suffices him.** [7]

So even on the demanding view, forgetting costs you nothing.

**What to do**: say *bismillah* as you begin. It takes a second, it satisfies
every position, and if it slips your mind halfway through, say it then and carry
on.
