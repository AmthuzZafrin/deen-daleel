# How do I make wudu with a cast or a bandage?

## Answer

You wipe over it. The law has a settled category for this — *jabira*, the splint
or dressing — and it is designed so that a broken arm does not put you out of
prayer.

**The rule in outline.** Wash every limb you can wash normally. When you reach
the one under the cast or dressing, **wipe over the covering** instead of washing
what is beneath it, and carry on. Where the injury is such that even wiping is
harmful, the Shafi'is add *tayammum* alongside the wudu for that limb — al-Nawawi
discusses at length whether the tayammum comes before, after or between the
washing, and the position most of the school settled on is **that he does not
move from one limb until he has completed its purification.** [1]

**Three practical points from al-Nawawi's discussion.**

*You do not have to redo it every prayer.* He raises the question — **must the
wearer of a dressing repeat the wudu for every obligatory prayer even if nothing
has broken it, as the tayammum must be repeated? Or does washing what is beyond
the dressing suffice? Or is nothing required so long as no impurity occurs? There
are three positions.** [2] And he settles it: **the sound view is that nothing at
all is required**, with Imam al-Haramayn and others reporting agreement on the
point. [3] Your wudu lasts as anyone else's does.

*When the cast comes off, wash what was under it.* **When the wearer of a
dressing recovers, washing the place of it becomes obligatory on him.** [4] Not
the prayers you prayed meanwhile — the limb, from then on.

*And it is not only for casts.* Al-Nawawi extends it: **all of this is where the
break required the dressing and he applied it; but if he did not need to apply
one, and merely fears letting water reach the limb, his ruling is that of the
wounded person** [5] — which is the same accommodation. A burn, stitches, an
open wound with no dressing on it: same chapter, same relief.

**What this means for you in the morning.**

1. Perform wudu normally.
2. At the covered limb, pass a wet hand over the cast, bandage, or dressing.
3. If wiping the covering would itself cause harm, follow your school on adding
   tayammum for that limb — and if you do not have a school yet, doing both is
   safe.
4. Pray. Do not repeat the wudu for the next prayer unless something broke it.

**Where to ask a person rather than a page**: if the covering extends well beyond
the injury — a full-arm cast for a wrist fracture — the schools discuss whether
the excess must be washed or whether the whole thing may be wiped. That turns on
your particular cast, and a scholar who can look at it will answer better than
this can.
