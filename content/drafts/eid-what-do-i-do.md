# What am I supposed to do on Eid?

## Answer

There is a short, specific morning routine, and then the rest of the day is yours.

Al-Quduri sets out the morning of Eid al-Fitr in one sentence: **it is recommended on the day of Fitr that a person eat before going out to the place of prayer, and wash, and apply perfume, and set off for the place of prayer** [1]. Eating first is the distinctive bit — it marks that the fasting is over. On Eid al-Adha the order reverses: **it is recommended on the day of Adha that he wash and apply perfume and delay eating until he has finished the prayer** [2].

Get there early and walk if you can. Ibn Qudama recommends **going out to the Eid on foot, with calm and dignity** [3], citing ibn Umar that the Prophet **used to go out to the Eid walking, and return walking** [4]. He also has Abu Sa'id's report that **the Prophet used to go out on the day of Fitr and of Adha to the place of prayer, and the first thing he would begin with was the prayer** [5] — no other prayer before it, and nothing else first.

On the way, say the takbir aloud. Ibn Qudama: **he says the takbir on the road to the Eid, and raises his voice with the takbir** [6], and quotes Ahmad: **he says the takbir aloud when he leaves his house until he reaches the place of prayer** [7]. Al-Quduri gives the wording used after the prescribed prayers in these days: *Allahu akbar, Allahu akbar, la ilaha illa Llah, wa-Llahu akbar, Allahu akbar, wa li-Llahi l-hamd.*

The prayer itself is short and you do not need to prepare for it. **The imam prays two units with the people** [8], with extra takbirs — three additional in the first unit and three in the second, in the Hanafi arrangement, with variations between the schools; follow the imam and you will be fine. There is no call to prayer. Its time is **from the sun's rising up until midday** [9], so it is an early-morning affair. Afterwards **he gives two sermons after the prayer, teaching the people in them about the charity of fitr and its rules** [10] — which is a reminder that zakat al-fitr should already have been paid before you arrived.

If you miss the congregation, al-Quduri notes there is no making it up — it is not a prayer you owe.

Beyond that, the day is for eating, seeing people, giving gifts, and not fasting. It is worth saying, if this is your first Eid and you have no one to spend it with: go to the prayer anyway, stay afterwards, and tell someone it is your first. That is usually all it takes.
