# Does touching yourself break wudu?

## Answer

**Genuinely disputed, and both sides go back to Companions.** Ibn Qudama sets out
the scope first: **the *farj* is the name for the outlet of impurity, taking in
the male organ, the anus, and the woman's front — and there is disagreement about
all of it breaking wudu, inside the school and outside it.** [1]

**Yes, it breaks wudu.** Ibn Qudama's first report from Ahmad, shared by **Ibn
'Umar, Sa'id b. al-Musayyib, 'Ata', Aban b. 'Uthman, 'Urwa, Sulayman b. Yasar,
al-Zuhri, al-Awza'i and al-Shafi'i — and it is the well-known position of
Malik.** [2] Their text is the hadith of Busra bint Safwan: **whoever touches his
penis, let him perform wudu.** [3] Ibn Qudama notes the grading: **al-Bukhari
said the soundest thing in this chapter is the hadith of Busra** [4] — and Ahmad
and al-Tirmidhi agreed it is sound.

**No, it does not.** The second report from Ahmad, and the position of **'Ali,
'Ammar, Ibn Mas'ud, Hudhayfa, 'Imran b. Husayn and Abu al-Darda' — and of
Rabi'a, al-Thawri, Ibn al-Mundhir and the people of opinion** (the Hanafis). [5]
Their text is the hadith of Talq b. 'Ali: a man asked, **what do you say about a
man touching his penis after he has performed wudu? He said: is it anything but
a piece of you?** [6]

So there are two sound-looking reports pointing opposite ways, each held by
Companions. This is not a case where one side is careless.

**What the "yes" side requires in practice** is direct contact with the hand —
not through clothing, and not the back of the hand on the schools that specify
it. Ibn Qudama's chapter also confines the dispute to the two outlets themselves;
nothing else on the body is in question.

**What to do.**

- *Shafi'i, Maliki, or the well-known Hanbali position*: renew wudu after direct
  contact with the palm or fingers. In practice this means after using the toilet
  you have already renewed wudu anyway.
- *Hanafi*: it does not break wudu.
- *Undecided*: washing your hands and renewing costs a minute and satisfies both.

**And it does not require ghusl**, on any position. Nor does it invalidate a
prayer you already prayed on the other view.
