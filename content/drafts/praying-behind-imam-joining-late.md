# I joined the prayer late — how do I finish?

## Answer

**Join immediately, wherever the imam is. When he gives the salam, stand up and
complete what you missed on your own.**

**The one thing to get right: what you "caught".** You have caught a rak'ah if you
joined in time to bow *with* the imam. If you arrive while he is already rising
from ruku', that rak'ah is not yours — join anyway, follow him, and make it up at
the end.

**The technical distinction the books draw** is worth knowing, because it explains
why you recite in some rak'ahs and not others. Ibn Nujaym divides latecomers into
two kinds: **the one who caught it is the one who caught all the rak'ahs with the
imam** [1], and **the *lahiq* who is not *masbuq* is the one who caught the first
rak'ah and then missed a rak'ah or more of it for an excuse — such as sleep, or
losing wudu, or inattention, or a crush** [2].

The *masbuq* — you, arriving late — is different from both. Al-Kasani: **as for the
*lahiq*, he performs what the imam preceded him in, then follows him** [3]; whereas
**the *masbuq* is praying individually, because he only committed to following the
imam for as much as he caught — do you not see that he recites and prostrates for
his own forgetfulness, unlike the *lahiq*?** [4]

That is the practical rule: **in the rak'ahs you make up after the imam finishes,
you recite al-Fatiha and a sura yourself, and you make your own prostration of
forgetfulness if you need one.**

**Step by step**

1. **Say the takbir and join**, whatever the imam is doing. Do not wait for him to
   stand.
2. **Follow him** for the rest of the prayer.
3. **Sit when he sits** for the tashahhud, even if it is not your own second
   rak'ah.
4. **When he gives the salam, do not.** Stand up and continue alone.
5. **Complete the number you missed**, reciting al-Fatiha and a short sura in the
   first two of them.
6. **Sit, do the tashahhud, and give the salam yourself.**

**A worked example.** You arrive at Maghrib as the imam rises from the second
rak'ah's bowing. You joined for one rak'ah's worth of following but caught no
bowing, so you have caught nothing yet; you follow through the third rak'ah, which
*is* yours. When he gives the salam you stand, pray two more rak'ahs with recitation
in both, sit, and finish.

**What you caught, technically.** The Hanafis hold: **Abu Hanifa and Abu Yusuf
said, what he caught with the imam is the end of his prayer in ruling, even if it
is the beginning of his prayer in reality** [5]. That is why you recite in the
make-up rak'ahs — they are treated as the *first* ones, which is where recitation
belongs.

**Two practical points**

*Do not rush in.* Walk. The instruction in the well-known hadith is to come to
prayer walking calmly and pray what you catch, then complete what you missed.

*Do not skip joining because you are late.* Even catching the final sitting gets
you the congregation. Join.

**And if you are unsure how much you missed**, build on what you are certain of and
make the prostration of forgetfulness — there is a separate answer here on that.
