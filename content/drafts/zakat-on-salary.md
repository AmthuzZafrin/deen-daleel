# Do I pay zakat on my salary?

## Answer

Not as it arrives, no. Zakat is not a payroll deduction and there is no monthly version of it. What you pay zakat on is what is *left* of your salary, sitting in your accounts, when your zakat year comes round.

The question the classical texts actually argue about is close to yours: what happens to money that comes in partway through a year you have already started. They call it *al-mustafad*, wealth newly acquired.

The majority answer is that money of a different kind starts its own clock. Ibn Qudama: **that the newly acquired is of a different kind from what he has — this has its own ruling, and is not joined to what he has, neither in year nor in nisab** [1]. A minority went further and had you pay on the spot: **it is related from ibn Mas'ud, ibn Abbas and Mu'awiya that zakat is due on it at the moment he acquires it** [2] — which is the closest thing in the sources to zakat on income as income. Ibn Qudama records it and rejects it: **the majority of scholars are against this view** [3].

Where money of the *same* kind is concerned — and cash added to cash is the same kind — the schools split, and the Hanafi answer is the one that matches how salaries actually work. **Abu Hanifa said he joins it to what he has in the year, and pays zakat on both together at the completion of the year of the wealth he already had** [4]. Ibn Abidin fills in the detail, including the case where the addition is what carries you over the line: **if the nisab was deficient and was completed by the newly acquired, the year begins over it at the point of completion** [5]. He also notes the pooling rule that makes this workable at all: **one of the two currencies is joined to the other, and trade goods are joined to the two currencies for their being of a kind, by their value** [6].

So the practical method is simple, and it is the same one everyone actually uses:

1. Pick one date in the year — many people use a date in Ramadan, purely for the reminder.
2. On that date, total your cash, bank balances and gold. That total already includes whatever survived of every pay packet.
3. If it is at or above the nisab, pay 2.5% of it.
4. Do the same on that date every year.

You do not track individual pay packets, and you do not owe anything on money you earned and spent. A high salary that leaves nothing at year end carries no zakat; a modest one that accumulates does. That is the whole logic of it — zakat falls on held wealth, not on income.

انتهى والذي يظهر أن المراد القدر المشترك بين الأمرين فمن امتثل بالإقرار أو بالفعل كفاه أو بهما فأولى وقد وقع في رواية الفضل بن العلاء بعد ذكر الصلاة فإذا صلوا وبعد ذكر الزكاة فإذا أقروا بذلك فخذ منهم (قوله صدقة) زاد في رواية أبي عاصم عن زكريا في أموالهم كما تقدم في أول الزكاة وفي رواية الفضل بن العلاء افترض عليهم زكاة في أموالهم تؤخذ من غنيهم فترد على فقيرهم (قوله تؤخذ من أغنيائهم) استدل به على أن الإمام هو الذي يتولى قبض الزكاة وصرفها إما بنفسه وإما بنائبه فمن أمتنع منها أخذت منه قهرا (قوله على فقرائهم) استدل به لقول مالك وغيره إنه يكفي إخراج الزكاة في صنف واحد وفيه بحث كما قال ابن دقيق العيد لاحتمال أن يكون ذكر الفقراء لكونهم الغالب في ذلك وللمطابقة بينهم وبين الأغنياء وقال الخطابي وقد يستدل به من لا يرى على المديون زكاة ما في يده إذا لم يفضل من الدين
