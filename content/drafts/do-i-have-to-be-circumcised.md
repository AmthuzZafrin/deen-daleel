# Do I have to be circumcised as an adult convert?

## Answer

Not to be Muslim. You already are, and nothing here changes that.

Beyond that, the schools genuinely differ, and it is worth having the actual
positions rather than whichever one you were told.

**The Hanafis hold it a sunna.** Al-Fatawa al-Hindiyya reports the disagreement
and settles it: **they differed over circumcision; it is said to be a sunna, and
that is the sound view.** [1]

**The Shafi'is hold it obligatory.** Al-Nawawi reports that al-Shafi'i, and the
bulk of the school with him, **held it obligatory**. [2] He also records when
that obligation is held to fall due: **after puberty**. [3] So on this view an
adult convert is inside the ruling, not outside it.

**The Hanbali report in Al-Mughni sides with the first.** Ibn Qudama transmits,
through al-Khallal from Shaddad b. Aws, that **circumcision is a sunna for men**.
[4]

**Now the part that was written for your situation.** Al-Fatawa al-Hindiyya
addresses the adult who enters Islam and cannot bear the procedure, and the
ruling is unambiguous: **if those with the relevant expertise say he cannot bear
it, it is left — because leaving an obligation for a valid excuse is permitted,
so leaving a sunna is the more so.** [5] The same passage adds that for an adult,
if he can manage it himself he does, and otherwise he does not. [6]

Notice what that reasoning does. It does not carve out an exception grudgingly;
it argues from the greater to the lesser — if hardship excuses a *duty*, it
certainly excuses a recommendation. The category the jurists put an adult convert
in is the category of someone with an excuse.

**What this means in practice.** This is a medical decision as much as a legal
one, and the passage above is explicit that it turns on what people who actually
examine you say. If you are considering it, the sequence is a doctor first and a
scholar of your school second — and the scholar will want to know what the doctor
said. Nobody can hand you a ruling on your body from a book.

And nothing in any of the positions above makes this urgent, or a condition of
prayer, or something to sort out before you can call yourself Muslim.
