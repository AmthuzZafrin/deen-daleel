# What are a husband's and wife's rights over each other?

## Answer

The Qur'an frames it as reciprocal before it frames it as anything else: **"And they (women) have rights
similar to those (of men) over them in kindness, and men are a degree above them"** [1]. Both halves are
in one sentence, and the first comes first.

### What she is owed

A man asked the Prophet ﷺ directly what a wife's right over her husband is, and got a list. **"That you
feed her when you eat, and clothe her when you clothe yourself; and that you not strike the face, nor
revile her, nor keep apart from her except in the house"** [2].

Five things, and three of them are prohibitions on him. The collection glosses the third: reviling means
**"that you say: may God make you ugly"** [3] — an ordinary insult, named and forbidden.

The material side is more than food and clothing. Ibn Qudama: **"she is owed from him all of her needs —
food, drink, clothing, and housing"** [4]. Al-Muhallab, cited by al-Qurtubi: **"maintenance of the wife
and dependants is obligatory by consensus"** [5]. And the priority is fixed by the hadith: **"the best
charity is what leaves independence behind, and the upper hand is better than the lower — and begin with
those you support"** [6]. Money given to a mosque before the household has been provided for has the
order wrong.

### What he is owed

The same hadith collection carries the other side. Al-Nawawi cites Muslim's version, in which a wife
**"does not admit anyone into his house except with his permission"** [7] — a right over the household
and its privacy, not over her person.

Beyond that: her companionship, her fidelity, her availability to him as his is to her, and her not
undermining the marriage. The classical books state the husband's rights more briefly than the wife's,
because most of what he is owed is not enforceable, and the jurists knew it.

### The measure of a man, in this law

The Prophet ﷺ set it explicitly, and the standard is domestic: **"the best of you is the best of you to
his family"** [8] — and he added, **"and I am the best of you to my family"** [9]. That is not a
consolation line appended to the rules. It is the criterion.

Which is why the classical discussion of the verse on *nushuz* runs the way it does. Al-Shafi'i reads it
as strictly staged: **"God Almighty said, 'and those whose defiance you fear' — it bears the meaning that
if he sees indications of the woman's plunging into and turning toward defiance, so that there is a place
for fear, he admonishes her; then if she manifests defiance, he withdraws from her; then if she persists
in it, he strikes her"** [10]. And it ends the moment the cause
ends: **"if the defiant wife returns from her defiance, her husband may not withdraw from her nor strike
her, because those two were permitted only by the defiance"** [11].

More telling is what the Prophet ﷺ did with the permission. He first said: **"do not strike the
handmaids of God"** [12]. 'Umar came and said women had become bold toward their husbands, and
permission was given — and then **"many women went round the family of Muhammad tonight" — or he said
"seventy women" — "all of them complaining. You will not find those men to be the best of you"** [13].
Al-Shafi'i draws the conclusion himself: **"so He gave them the striking and gave them the pardoning,
and informed them that the better course is to leave it"** [14].

Read [12], [13] and [14] together with [2] — which forbids the face outright — and [8]. A man who uses
that verse as a licence has been told, by the same Prophet who conveyed it, that he is not among the best
of you.

### In practice

Rights in this law are things you owe, not things you collect. The verse [1] makes them mutual and the
hadith [8] makes the standard generosity rather than entitlement. A marriage where both parties are
studying their own obligations works; one where both are auditing the other's does not, whatever the
fiqh says.

If a marriage has reached the point where the question is what can be enforced, that is a matter for a
scholar or a mediator who knows the people involved — and if there is violence in it, for the authorities
first. Nothing above should be read as a reason to stay in danger.
