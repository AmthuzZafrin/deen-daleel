# How many days can I keep shortening prayers when I'm away?

## Answer

**This is one of the most genuinely disputed questions in the chapter, and the
range is wide — from four days to fifteen, with an unlimited case at the end.**

**The positions**

| | how long you may keep shortening |
|---|---|
| **Maliki, Shafi'i** | up to 4 days |
| **Hanbali** | more than 21 prayers — a little over 4 days |
| **Hanafi (and al-Thawri)** | up to 15 days |
| **All** | **indefinitely**, if you have not settled on a length of stay |

Ibn Qudama lays them out. His own school: **the well-known position from Ahmad is
that the period which obliges the traveller to pray in full, by intending to stay
for it, is what is more than twenty-one prayers.** [1] The second Hanbali
narration, and the majority: **if he intends a stay of four days he prays in full,
and if he intends less than that he shortens** [2] — **and this is the position of
Malik, al-Shafi'i and Abu Thawr** [3]. And the Hanafis: **al-Thawri and the People
of Opinion said: if he stays fifteen days, counting the day he leaves, he prays in
full; and if he intends less than that, he shortens.** [4]

**The rule that matters most is the last one.** Ibn Qudama: **whoever heads for a
particular town and arrives there without having resolved to stay for a period that
would end the ruling of his journey — he may shorten there.** [5]

This is the case most travellers are actually in, and it is why the four-versus-
fifteen dispute matters less than it looks. If you do not know how long you will be
somewhere — waiting on a visa, a job, a court date, a family situation, a flight —
you keep shortening, **however long it actually turns out to be**. The clock is
started by an *intention to stay*, not by elapsed time.

The evidence Ahmad reasons from is the Prophet's own stay in Mecca, which the
Companions counted at ten, eighteen or nineteen days depending on the report,
shortening throughout — because **the Prophet did not resolve upon staying** [6].

**Practically**

- **Short trip, known length under 4 days** — shorten, on every school.
- **4 to 15 days, known length** — depends on your school. Hanafi: shorten.
  Others: pray in full from arrival.
- **Length genuinely unknown** — shorten, on all of them, for as long as it lasts.
- **You live there now** — you are not a traveller. Pray in full.
- **Unsure which school to follow?** Praying in full is always valid; shortening is
  the concession. If in doubt on a stay of a week or more, pray in full and you
  are safe on every position.

**Two clarifications**

*The count is of your intention, not of the days.* Someone who arrives intending
two days and ends up staying three weeks, extending a day at a time, keeps
shortening on the reasoning above.

*Working away for months is not travelling.* If you have a room, a routine and a
return date months off, you have effectively taken up residence, whatever the
technical count. Pray in full.

**And when you are unsure**, the safe act is the complete prayer. Nobody has ever
been faulted for praying four.

وأما إسحاق فرأى أقوى المذاهب فيه حديث ابن عباس، قال: لأنه روى عن النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم ثم تأوله بعد النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم: إذا أجمع على إقامة تسع عشرة أتم الصلاة.
