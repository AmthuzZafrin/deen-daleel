# Do I have to go to a mosque to convert?

## Answer

No. You can become Muslim where you are standing.

Ibn 'Abidin, setting out the question directly, divides what makes a person
Muslim into two kinds — **speech and act** — and says the speech is the two words
of testimony. [1] Not a speech made in a particular building, and not one made in
front of particular people. The condition is on what is said, and on meaning it.

There is an old dispute among the jurists that settles the point about place
almost by accident. The question was whether a non-Muslim who *prays* has thereby
entered Islam. Ibn Qudama gives the Hanbali answer as yes — and note the terms he
uses to say so: **whether in the abode of war or the abode of Islam, whether in
congregation or alone**. [2] He then reports al-Shafi'i disagreeing, but on
whether the prayer might be dissimulation, not on where it happened. When the
sources argue about what makes someone Muslim, they argue about the act and about
sincerity. The venue is not among the things they think worth disputing.

The Prophet's own call bears this out. Ibn Hajar, explaining the letter sent to
Heraclius, glosses its phrase *the summons of Islam* as **the word that calls to
Islam, which is the testimony that there is no god but God and that Muhammad**
is His messenger. [3] The invitation was carried to a man in a palace in
Byzantium. It was not an invitation to travel somewhere first.

**One condition is real, and it is not about location.** Ibn Qudama holds that
where someone is coerced into Islam who should not have been, it does not take
effect — not until something shows they hold to it *willingly*. [4] What makes it
count is that it is yours. A room on your own at two in the morning satisfies
that better than most mosques would.

**Why go to a mosque anyway.** Not to become Muslim — for everything after. Being
shown how to pray by someone standing next to you is worth more than any
description, and a mosque can usually give you a certificate if you ever need to
prove your Islam for a marriage, a burial or a Hajj visa. Those are good reasons
and they will keep.

And if the mosque is the obstacle — if it is far, or the wrong language, or you
have walked past it four times without going in — that is a real difficulty and
a common one, but it is not standing between you and Islam. It never was.

### Passage 9 — Qur'an 5:2

- kind: quran · source: The Meaning of the Glorious Koran · chunk: 7402

```
Qur'an 5:2 — Surah Al-Maaida, verse 2 of chapter 5

Translation (Marmaduke Pickthall):
O ye who believe! Profane not Allah's monuments nor the Sacred Month nor the offerings nor the garlands, nor those repairing to the Sacred House, seeking the grace and pleasure of their Lord. But when ye have left the sacred territory, then go hunting (if ye will). And let not your hatred of a folk who (once) stopped your going to the inviolable place of worship seduce you to transgress; but help ye one another unto righteousness and pious duty. Help not one another unto sin and transgression, but keep your duty to Allah. Lo! Allah is severe in punishment.

### Passage 11 — Qur'an 2:189

- kind: quran · source: The Meaning of the Glorious Koran · chunk: 6927

```
Qur'an 2:189 — Surah Al-Baqara, verse 189 of chapter 2

Translation (Marmaduke Pickthall):
They ask thee, (O Muhammad), of new moons, say: They are fixed seasons for mankind and for the pilgrimage. It is not righteousness that ye go to houses by the backs thereof (as do the idolaters at certain seasons), but the righteous man is he who wardeth off (evil). So go to houses by the gates thereof, and observe your duty to Allah, that ye may be successful.

- kind: quran · source: The Meaning of the Glorious Koran · chunk: 11859

Qur'an 59:2 — Surah Al-Hashr, verse 2 of chapter 59

Translation (Marmaduke Pickthall):
He it is Who hath caused those of the People of the Scripture who disbelieved to go forth from their homes unto the first exile. Ye deemed not that they would go forth, while they deemed that their strongholds would protect them from Allah. But Allah reached them from a place whereof they recked not, and cast terror in their hearts so that they ruined their houses with their own hands and the hands of the believers. So learn a lesson, O ye who have eyes!
