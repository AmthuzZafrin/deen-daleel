# What is zakat al-fitr and when do I pay it?

## Answer

It is a small charity of food paid by every Muslim household at the end of Ramadan, before the Eid prayer. It is not the same thing as ordinary zakat: it is owed per person, not on wealth, and the amounts are tiny.

It is obligatory. Ibn Qudama quotes ibn al-Mundhir: **every one of the people of knowledge whose view is preserved agreed that the charity of fitr is an obligation** [1]. The Hanafis grade the same duty slightly differently — al-Sarakhsi says **with us it is wajib** [2], a category one notch below *fard* in their terminology — but the practical upshot is identical: you pay it.

The founding report is ibn Umar's: the Prophet **made the charity of fitr obligatory upon every free person and slave, male or female, young or old** [3]. So it is per head, and it includes children. Al-Quduri sets out who actually hands it over: **he pays it for himself and for his young children** [4].

There is a threshold. **The charity of fitr is obligatory upon a free Muslim if he owns the amount of the nisab, over and above his dwelling, his clothes and his furniture** [5]. If you are genuinely poor, you are a recipient, not a payer.

The amount is stated in food. Al-Quduri: **the fitra is half a sa' of wheat, or a sa' of dates, raisins or barley** [6]. Abu Sa'id al-Khudri described what they actually gave: **we used to give out the zakat of fitr — a sa' of food, or a sa' of barley, or a sa' of dates, or a sa' of dry cheese, or a sa' of raisins** [7]. A sa' is a volume measure of roughly 2.5 to 3 kg depending on the commodity, and mosques normally announce a cash equivalent each year. Take theirs rather than calculating your own.

The timing is the part people get wrong. It becomes due at dawn: **the obligation of the fitra attaches to the rising of dawn on the day of Fitr** [8]. And it must reach the poor before the prayer — ibn Umar reports that the Prophet **ordered that the zakat of fitr be paid before the people go out to the prayer** [9]. Al-Quduri gives the full window and the consequence of missing it: **it is recommended that people pay the fitra on the day of Fitr before going out to the place of prayer; if they pay it in advance of the day of Fitr that is permitted; and if they delay it beyond the day of Fitr it does not lapse, and they are obliged to pay it** [10]. So paying a few days early is fine and is what most mosques ask for; paying late is a failing but does not cancel the debt.

Practically: count the people in your household, including children, multiply by whatever your local mosque has announced, and pay it in the last week of Ramadan so it actually reaches someone before Eid morning.

(984) حدثنا عبد الله بن مسلمة بن قعنب، وقتيبة بن سعيد، قالا: حدثنا مالك، ح وحدثنا يحيى بن يحيى، - واللفظ له - قال: قرأت على مالك، عن نافع، عن ابن عمر: «أن رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم فرض زكاة الفطر من رمضان على الناس، صاعا من تمر، أو صاعا من شعير، على كل حر أو عبد، ذكر أو أنثى، من المسلمين»

(984) حدثنا قتيبة بن سعيد، حدثنا ليث، ح وحدثنا محمد بن رمح، أخبرنا الليث، عن نافع، أن عبد الله بن عمر، قال: " إن رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم: أمر بزكاة الفطر صاع من تمر، أو صاع من شعير " قال ابن عمر: «فجعل الناس عدله مدين من حنطة»

16 -

(984) وحدثنا محمد بن رافع، حدثنا ابن أبي فديك، أخبرنا الضحاك، عن نافع، عن عبد الله بن عمر: «أن رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم فرض زكاة الفطر من رمضان على كل نفس من المسلمين حر، أو عبد، أو رجل، أو امرأة، صغير أو كبير صاعا من تمر، أو صاعا من شعير»

17 -

(985) حدثنا يحيى بن يحيى، قال: قرأت على مالك، عن زيد بن أسلم، عن عياض بن عبد الله بن سعد بن أبي سرح، أنه سمع أبا سعيد الخدري، يقول: «كنا نخرج زكاة الفطر صاعا من طعام، أو صاعا من شعير، أو صاعا من تمر، أو صاعا من أقط، أو صاعا من زبيب»
