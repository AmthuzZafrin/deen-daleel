# What are the six fasts of Shawwal?

## Answer

Six voluntary fasts in Shawwal, the month that begins with Eid al-Fitr, taken on the strength of one hadith from Abu Ayyub al-Ansari: **whoever fasts Ramadan and then follows it with six from Shawwal, it is as though he fasted a lifetime** [1]. Al-Tirmidhi records it in nearly the same words and grades it sound: **whoever fasts Ramadan and then follows it with six from Shawwal, that is the fast of a lifetime** [2].

The arithmetic behind the promise is spelled out in a second report Ibn Qudama cites from Thawban: **whoever fasts Ramadan, a month counts as ten months; and he fasts six days after the Fitr, and that completes a year** [3]. A good deed is repaid tenfold, so thirty days become three hundred and six become sixty — three hundred and sixty, a full year. Ibn Taymiyya makes the same point and adds the important qualification: **if he fasts Ramadan and six from Shawwal, the total earns him the reward of fasting a lifetime** [4] — the reward, without the harm that actually fasting every day would bring.

How to space them is open. Ibn Qudama: **there is no difference between their being consecutive or spread out, at the beginning of the month or at its end** [5], because the hadith is unrestricted. Al-Tirmidhi notes that **ibn al-Mubarak preferred that the six days be at the beginning of the month** [6], but also reports him saying that **if he fasts six days of Shawwal spread out, it is permissible** [7]. So any six days of Shawwal will do.

You should know that one imam disliked it. Ibn Qudama reports Malik's position honestly: **Malik disliked it and said: I have not seen anyone of the people of jurisprudence fasting them** [8] — his fear being that people would attach to Ramadan something that is not part of it. That objection is recorded, considered, and not followed by the majority.

Two practical points. If you owe makeup days from Ramadan, most scholars would have you fast those first; the six are voluntary and the debt is not. And you cannot fast the day of Eid itself — Shawwal begins with a day on which fasting is forbidden, so the six start from the second.
