# Is there a deadline for making up missed fasts?

## Answer

Yes, and it is generous: the next Ramadan. Ibn Qudama states the rule plainly — whoever owes days from Ramadan **may delay them so long as the next Ramadan has not come in** [1]. That gives you roughly eleven months.

The precedent is Aisha's own practice. She said: **I used to owe fasts from Ramadan, and I would not make them up until Sha'ban came** [2] — Sha'ban being the month immediately before Ramadan. Al-Tirmidhi records her in almost the same words and grades the report sound: **I never used to make up what I owed from Ramadan except in Sha'ban** [3]. So delaying almost the whole year is not blameworthy; it is documented of the Prophet's own household.

Delaying past the next Ramadan is a different matter. Ibn Qudama: **it is not permissible for him to delay the making-up until another Ramadan without an excuse** [4] — and he reasons from Aisha herself, that she did not delay it that far, and would have if she could. If you do let it slip past, the consequence depends on why. **If it was for an excuse, he owes nothing but the making-up; and if it was without excuse, he owes, along with the making-up, the feeding of one poor person for each day** [5]. That extra feeding is the position of ibn Abbas, ibn Umar, Abu Hurayra, Malik, al-Thawri, al-Awza'i, al-Shafi'i and Ishaq.

The Hanafis disagree about the penalty, not the priority. Al-Kasani reports it as settled in his school that **if he delays making up Ramadan until another Ramadan comes in, he owes no expiatory feeding** [6], on the reasoning that the feeding stands in for fasting only when someone is genuinely unable to fast, and this person is able. Behind that lies their view that the command to make up is **unrestricted as to time, and it may not be tied down to particular times without evidence** [7], so that the obligation is **on a relaxed timetable in the view of our teachers generally** [8] rather than immediate.

And if years have gone by? Ibn Qudama caps it: **if he delayed without excuse until two Ramadans or more overtook him, he owes no more than one feeding along with the making-up** [9]. The debt does not compound. Whatever school you follow, the practical shape is the same — count the days, start fasting them, and if you are past the deadline, feed a poor person per day alongside if you are able and can do so without hardship.
