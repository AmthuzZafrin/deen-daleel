# How do I make wudu, step by step?

## Answer

The Qur'an gives the obligation in one sentence, and a Companion gave the
demonstration. Both are below; the demonstration is what you should follow.

**What is commanded.** *When ye rise up for prayer, wash your faces, and your
hands up to the elbows, and lightly rub your heads and wash your feet up to the
ankles.* [1] Four things: face washed, arms washed to the elbows, head wiped,
feet washed to the ankles.

**How it was actually done.** 'Uthman called for water and performed it in front
of people so they could see. Sahih Muslim records the sequence: **he washed his
hands three times, then rinsed his mouth and nose, then washed his face three
times, then his right arm to the elbow three times, then his left the same, then
wiped his head, then washed his right foot to the ankles three times, then the
left the same.** [2] He then said: **I saw the Messenger of God perform wudu like
this wudu of mine.** [3]

So, in order:

1. **Hands** — wash both, three times.
2. **Mouth and nose** — rinse the mouth, draw water into the nose and expel it.
   'Abd Allah b. Zayd's demonstration in Bukhari has this as **three scoops.** [4]
3. **Face** — three times, from hairline to chin, ear to ear.
4. **Right arm to the elbow**, three times. Then the left.
5. **Head** — wipe it. Bukhari records the motion: **he wiped his head with both
   hands, forward and back — beginning at the front of his head, taking them to
   the nape, then returning them to where he began.** [5]
6. **Right foot to the ankle**, three times. Then the left.

That is the complete form. Ibn Shihab notes what his teachers said about it:
**this is the most ample wudu anyone performs for prayer.** [6]

**Not all of it is obligatory.** The rinsing of mouth and nose, the three
repetitions, the right-before-left — these are the sunna additions that make the
wudu complete, not the parts without which it is invalid. Which is which is the
next question in this collection, and worth reading, because it is the difference
between a wudu that is less than ideal and one that has to be done again.

**What the Prophet attached to it.** In the same report: **whoever performs wudu
like this wudu of mine, then stands and prays two units without letting his mind
wander in them, is forgiven what came before of his sin.** [7]

And the verse ends by saying what the whole thing is for: **God would not place a
burden on you, but He would purify you.** [8] If you are standing at a sink
counting and worrying, that sentence is the correction.

- found via: "فرائض الوضوء الأربعة والترتيب والموالاة"

```
Fath al-Bari 11:359

Arabic:
وجوهم إضاءة القمر ليلة البدر) في رواية لمسلم على صورة القمر قال القرطبي المراد بالصورة الصفة يعني انهم في اشراق وجوهم على صفة القمر ليلة تمامه وهي ليلة أربعة عشر ويؤخذ منه أن أنوار أهل الجنة تتفاوت بحسب درجاتهم (قلت) وكذا صفاتهم في الجمال ونحوه (قوله يرفع نمرة عليه) بفتح النون وكسر الميم هي كساء من صوف كالشملة مخططة بسواد وبياض يلبسها الاعراب * الحديث الثالث (قوله أبو غسان) بغين معجمة ثم مهملة ثقيلة وأبو حازم هو سلمة بن دينار (قوله ليدخلن الجنة من أمتي سبعون ألفا أو سبعمائة ألف شك في أحدهما) في رواية مسلم من طريق عبد العزيز بن محمد عن أبي حازم لا يدري أبو حازم أيهما قال (قوله متماسكين) بالنصب على الحال وفي رواية مسلم متماسكون بالرفع على الصفة قال النووي كذا في معظم النسخ وفي بعضها بالنصب وكلاهما صحيح (قوله آخذ بعضهم ببعض) في رواية مسلم بعضهم بعضا (قوله حتى يدخل أولهم وآخرهم) هو غاية للتماسك المذكور والاخذ بالأيدي وفي رواية فضيل بن سليمان
```
