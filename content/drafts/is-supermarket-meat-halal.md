# Can I eat meat from a regular supermarket?

## Answer

**Short answer: it depends on two separate things — who slaughtered the animal,
and how — and the classical sources settle the first far more clearly than the
second.** The question everyone asks about ("did they say the name?") turns out
to be the one the scholars were most relaxed about. The question almost nobody
asks about — was the killing a valid slaughter at all? — is the one that actually
decides it in a modern plant.

**Start with what is definitely not the test.** The Qur'an's list of what is
forbidden is short, and none of its entries is "meat without a label": **"Forbidden
unto you (for food) are carrion and blood and swineflesh, and that which hath
been dedicated unto any other than Allah"** [1]. The default the surah opens with
runs the other way: **"O ye who believe! Fulfil your indentures. The beast of
cattle is made lawful unto you (for food) except that which is announced unto you
(herein)"** [2].

**The name at slaughter: four positions, in one sentence.** Al-Marghinani sets
them out compactly: **"قال وإن ترك الذابح التسمية عمدا فالذبيحة ميتة لا تؤكل وإن
تركها ناسيا أكل وقال الشافعي رحمه الله أكل في الوجهين وقال مالك رحمه الله لا يؤكل
في الوجهين والمسلم والكتابي في ترك التسمية سواء"** — *if the slaughterer omits
the naming deliberately, the animal is carrion and is not eaten; and if he omits
it forgetfully, it is eaten. Al-Shafi'i said: it is eaten in both cases. Malik
said: it is not eaten in either case. And the Muslim and the person of the
Scripture are the same regarding omission of the naming* [3].

Ibn Qudama gives the Hanbali position: **"وأما الذبيحة فالمشهور من مذهب أحمد،
أنها شرط مع الذكر، وتسقط بالسهو"** — *as for the slaughtered animal, the
well-known position of Ahmad's school is that it is a condition when remembered,
and lapses through forgetfulness* [4]. Ibn 'Abd al-Barr states al-Shafi'i's side
with its authorities: **"وقال الشافعي وأصحابه تؤكل الذبيحة والصيد في الوجهين
جميعا تعمد ذلك أو نسيه"** — *al-Shafi'i and his companions said the slaughtered
animal and the game are eaten in both cases, whether he did it deliberately or
forgot* [5] — reporting from Ibn 'Abbas and Abu Wa'il the terse argument **"إنما
ذبحت بدينك"** — *you slaughtered it with your religion* [6]. Ibn Hajar cites a
report he grades favourably: **"ذبيحة المسلم حلال ذكر اسم الله أو لم يذكر"** —
*the slaughter of a Muslim is lawful, whether he mentioned the name of Allah or
not* [7] — noting of its transmitter **"الصلت يقال له السدوسي ذكره ابن حبان في
الثقات وهو مرسل جيد"** — *al-Salt is called al-Sadusi; Ibn Hibban listed him
among the reliable, and it is a good mursal report* [8]. Even within the Maliki
school there is a softening: **"قال أشهب : تؤكل ذبيحة تارك التسمية عمدا إلا أن
يكون مستخفا"** — *Ashhab said: the slaughter of one who deliberately omits the
naming is eaten, unless he is being contemptuous* [9].

**And the decisive point for your situation: not knowing is not the same as
knowing it was omitted.** Ibn 'Abd al-Barr: **"أن ما ذبحه المسلم ولم يعرف هل سمى
الله عليه أم لا أنه لا بأس بأكله وهو محمول على أنه قد سمى والمؤمن لا يظن به إلا
الخير وذبيحته وصيده أبدا محمول على السلامة حتى يصح فيه غير ذلك"** — *what a
Muslim slaughtered, where it is not known whether he said the name of Allah over
it, there is no harm in eating it; it is presumed that he did. Nothing but good
is assumed of a believer, and his slaughtering and hunting are always presumed
sound until something else is established* [10].

**So where does that leave a supermarket?** Two questions, and only the first is
answered above.

**1. Who slaughtered it?** If a Muslim, or a Jew or Christian, the classical
permission covers it — see *Is meat slaughtered by Christians or Jews
permissible?*, where that is reported as agreed. If the answer is someone of
neither category, the permission does not reach it, and no amount of relaxation
about the naming helps.

**2. Was it slaughtered at all, in the sense the law means?** This is the modern
question, and none of the passages above touch it. An animal killed by a method
that leaves it dead before the cut, or by a process that does not sever what
*dhakah* requires, is not a case of "the name was omitted" — it is a case of the
animal being carrion, which the first verse settles the other way. See *What about
meat that was stunned before slaughter?*

**Practically.**

- **Find out what applies where you are.** In a country where meat is slaughtered
  overwhelmingly by Christians, question 1 answers itself and question 2 is the
  live one. Where the slaughterers are of neither category, question 1 already
  decides it.
- **Ask the retailer, not the internet.** What decides your case is a fact about a
  specific supply chain, and shops will usually tell you.
- **Do not treat "I do not know" as "it is forbidden."** The presumption above
  runs the other way, and the anxiety costs more than it protects.
- **Certified meat, or simply eating non-meat, are both ordinary answers** — and
  the second requires no ruling from anyone.
- **If you follow a particular school, its position on the naming is above**, and
  may narrow or widen what is open to you.

**Where this stops.** This site does not rule on any particular shop, brand,
country, or certification scheme, because that is a claim about facts it cannot
check. What it can tell you is which two questions to ask, and which of them the
sources have already answered.

### Passage 22 — Fath al-Bari 3:456

- kind: hadith · source: Fath al-Bari · chunk: 355108
- found via: "ترك التسمية على الذبيحة عمدا أو نسيانا واختلاف الفقهاء"

```
Fath al-Bari 3:456

Arabic:
فما سمعته سئل يومئذ عن أمر مما ينسى المرء أو يجهل من تقديم بعض الأمور على بعض أو أشباهها إلا قال افعلوا ذلك ولا حرج واحتج به وبقوله في رواية مالك لم أشعر بأن الرخصة تختص بمن نسي أو جهل لا بمن تعمد قال صاحب المغني قال الأثرم عن أحمد إن كان ناسيا أو جاهلا فلا شئ عليه وإن كان عالما فلا لقوله في الحديث لم أشعر وأجاب بعض الشافعية بأن الترتيب لو كان واجبا لما سقط بالسهو كالترتيب بين السعي والطواف فإنه لو سعى قبل أن يطوف وجب إعادة السعي وأما ما وقع في حديث أسامة بن شريك فمحمول على من سعى بعد طواف القدوم ثم طاف طواف الإفاضة فإنه يصدق عليه أنه سعى قبل الطواف أي طواف الركن ولم يقل بظاهر حديث أسامة إلا أحمد وعطاء فقالا لو لم يطف للقدوم ولا لغيره وقدم السعي قبل طواف الإفاضة أجزأه أخرجه عبد الرزاق عن ابن جريج عنه وقال ابن دقيق العيد ما قاله أحمد قوي من جهة أن الدليل دل على وجوب أتباع الرسول في الحج بقوله خذوا عني مناسككم وهذه الأحاديث المرخصة في تقديم ما وقع عنه تأخيره قد قرنت بقول السائل لم أشعر فيختص الحكم بهذه الحالة وتبقى حالة العمد على أصل وجوب الأتباع
```
