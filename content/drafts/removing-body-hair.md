# What are the rules about removing body hair?

## Answer

This belongs to the *fitra* — the practices of natural human decency the
prophets kept — and the rules are gentler and more flexible than people
usually expect. The short version: shave or remove the pubic and underarm hair,
by whatever method you can manage, roughly every few weeks; everything else is
largely left to you.

**The list.** Abu Hurayra's report: "the *fitra* is five — or five are of the
*fitra*: circumcision, shaving the pubic hair, plucking the underarm hair,
trimming the nails, and clipping..." [1]. The longer version names ten: "ten are
of the *fitra* — trimming the moustache, letting the beard grow, the *siwak*,
drawing water into the nose, clipping the nails, washing the finger joints,
plucking the underarm hair, shaving the pubic hair..." [2]. And the list is not
closed: "it is not confined to the ten — the Prophet ﷺ indicated that it is not
confined to them by his saying *of the fitra*" [3].

**Its weight.** Al-Khattabi, quoted by al-Nawawi: "most of the scholars held
that it means the *sunna* — and a group besides al-Khattabi said the same; they
said its meaning is that these are among the practices of the prophets" [4]. And
plainly on obligation: "most of these traits are not obligatory in the view of
the scholars, and over some of them there is disagreement as to their
obligation" [5]. So these are strong recommendations, not duties you are sinning
by delaying — with circumcision the disputed case.

**The pubic area.** Al-Nawawi: "*istihdad* is shaving the pubic hair — it was
called *istihdad* for the use of the iron blade, that is, the razor; and it is a
*sunna*, and what is intended by it is the cleanliness of that place. The best
in it is shaving, and it is permitted by clipping, by plucking and by depilatory
paste" [6]. That is the answer on waxing, creams, and trimmers: the *purpose* is
cleanliness of the area, the *preferred* method is shaving, and other methods
are explicitly allowed. The area meant: "the hair above a man's member and
around it, and likewise the hair around a woman's private part" [7].

**The underarms.** "Plucking the underarm hair is a *sunna* by agreement, and
the best in it is plucking for whoever is strong enough for it — and it is also
achieved by shaving and by depilatory paste" [8]. And then the most humane line
in the whole file, from Yunus b. 'Abd al-A'la: "I came in upon al-Shafi'i and
the barber was with him, shaving his underarm — and al-Shafi'i said: I know the
*sunna* is plucking, but I cannot bear the pain" [9]. An imam of a school
choosing the easier permitted method and saying so out loud. If that was
available to al-Shafi'i it is available to you.

**How often.** Anas's report: the Messenger of Allah ﷺ "set a time for us for
trimming the moustache, clipping the nails, shaving the pubic hair and plucking
the underarm hair — not to be left more than forty days" [10]. Al-Nawawi reads
it as a ceiling and not a schedule: "its meaning is that he should not leave it
in a way that exceeds forty — not that they were given forty as a term for
leaving it" [11]. And the more basic rule he gives is need: "it is regulated by
need and by its length — when it grows long, he shaves; and likewise the
regulation in trimming the moustache, plucking the underarm hair and clipping
the nails" [12].

Ibn 'Abd al-Barr notes the forty-day report is not the strongest: "this hadith
is not strong from the standpoint of transmission, but a group has held by it"
[13] — and records that "most of them hold that there is no fixed time in any of
it" [14]. So forty days is a well-known outer limit rather than a deadline you
have missed.

**On modesty while doing it.** Ibn 'Abbas: "the Prophet ﷺ had depilatory applied
— a man applied it to him and covered his 'awra with a cloth, and the man
applied it to the rest of his body; when he had finished the Prophet ﷺ said to
him: *leave me* — then the Prophet ﷺ applied it to his own 'awra with his own
hand" [15]. Another may help with the rest; that part is your own. Al-Nawawi
draws the same line: for the moustache "he may choose between trimming it
himself and delegating it to someone else, since the purpose is achieved without
violating dignity or sanctity — unlike the underarm and the pubic area" [16].

**And both methods are prophetic.** From Anas: "the Prophet ﷺ did not use
depilatory paste; when his hair grew long, he shaved it" [17] — while Ibn
'Abbas's report has him using paste. The commentators reconcile them as habit
against occasion. Either way, the method is not the point; the cleanliness is.
